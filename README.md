# Customer Churn Analysis (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes **customer churn behavior** to uncover insights into **customer retention, churn drivers, and high-risk segments** using interactive Power BI dashboards.

The goal is to support **data-driven decision-making** by identifying:
- Customer segments with the highest churn risk  
- Key factors influencing churn (tenure, contract, payment method, demographics)  
- Revenue exposure due to churn  
- Opportunities for targeted retention strategies  

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Analyze **overall churn rate and churn volume**
- Identify **high-risk customer segments**
- Understand churn patterns by **tenure, contract type, and payment method**
- Examine churn behavior across **demographics**
- Assess the **financial impact of churn**

## 🗂 Dataset Description
The dataset contains customer-level subscription data with the following attributes:
- Customer demographics (Age group, Gender, Marital status)
- Account information (Tenure, Contract type)
- Billing & payment details (Payment method, Paperless billing)
- Service information (Internet service)
- Financial metrics (Monthly charges, Total charges)
- Churn indicator (Yes / No)

## 🧠 Key Insights
- Customers on **month-to-month contracts** show the highest churn rate (~46%).
- **New customers (0–1 year tenure)** are significantly more likely to churn.
- **Mailed check** users have the highest churn risk among payment methods.
- Older customers (**66–80 age group**) exhibit elevated churn rates.
- Longer tenure and long-term contracts strongly correlate with **lower churn**.

---

## 📌 Recommendations
- Incentivize customers to switch to **long-term contracts**.
- Focus retention strategies on **customers within their first year**.
- Promote **digital payment methods** to reduce churn risk.
- Develop targeted engagement strategies for **high-risk demographic segments**.
- Use churn insights to guide **proactive customer retention campaigns**.
